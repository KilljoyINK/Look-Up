About In My Room

A star viewer that runs in your browser. Point your phone at the sky and see what's really there: about 5,000 stars, the Moon, and the planets. Everything is worked out on your phone, so it works with no internet.

Madot Girma

Madot Girma is a Mechanical Design Engineer based in Atlanta, GA. This Web App was developed for offline use to help users identify constellations and share stars they’ve found with others. Code is housed in Git.

Find Madot somewhere outside, looking up. Say hello @ mgirma@inkconsulting.xyz

This app needs your location

The sky looks different depending on where you stand. To show the right stars in the right places, This App needs to know roughly where you are.

  • ✓Used only on this phone to work out which stars are above you right now.
  • ✓Never sent anywhere or saved on a server. Not even when you send a star.
  • ✓Your phone will ask you to confirm. You can say no and pick a city instead.

Point your phone at the sky

For AR mode, This App uses two more things. Both stay on your phone.

  • ◎Motion & compass to know which way your phone is pointing.
  • ◉Camera (optional) to show stars over the real sky. Nothing is recorded.
